FAQ

Common questions

Start here if you are new to Sunsynk Reports.

Do I need a Sunsynk account?

Yes. Sign in with your existing Sunsynk email and password. The report uses the readings already linked to that account. Installers may also use the PDF when helping a homeowner.

Is this the official Sunsynk monitoring app?

No. Sunsynk’s own app shows live charts and system status. This service creates a separate plain-English PDF that summarises about 30 days of performance, optional savings, and what to do next.

What is inside the PDF?

A readable report with an overall score, data-quality checks, optional estimated savings (if you save your electricity rate), solar and battery insights, evening grid use, and a ranked list of recommended actions.

Is there a subscription?

No. One credit equals one report. Buy Starter (1), Classic (3), or Pro (6) when you need them. Credits never expire. Unused credits from a purchase can be refunded within 14 days.

Can I trust the numbers?

Before generation, the app checks about 30 days of data coverage and warns you if coverage is thin. Each PDF also shows coverage and accuracy checks, plus assumptions in Important Information.

How do I get a report?

Go to app.sunsynkreports.com, sign in with Sunsynk, buy credits if needed, optionally save your electricity rate under Settings, then generate a report for your system. When it is ready, preview and download the PDF. One credit is used per report.
